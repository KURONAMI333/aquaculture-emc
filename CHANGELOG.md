# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First release for NeoForge 1.21.1 (Aquaculture 2 2.7.21+, ProjectE 1.1.0+) and Forge 1.20.1 (Aquaculture 2 2.5.7+, ProjectE 1.0.1+).
- EMC for Aquaculture 2's fish, fillets, fish junk and neptunium: 39 items priced by hand on both cells. From those values ProjectE derives the cooked fillet, sushi, turtle soup, Neptune's Bounty, the neptunium block and nugget and the neptunium tools, armour, rods, knives and bows.
- Loot boxes, the worm farm's worm, the six hook assets with no item behind them, and the leech and jellyfish that brew vanilla potions are left unvalued.
